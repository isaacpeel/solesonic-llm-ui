About Izzy-Bot

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Izzy-Bot is an AI platform available by invite only, operated by Isaac Peel ("we", "us"). It uses self-hosted large language models to power a private chat assistant for invited users.

Connecting your Google account

When connected, Izzy-Bot accesses your Google account to sync your calendar schedules and manage your tasks. This lets Izzy-Bot read and update relevant events and to-dos on your behalf as part of the features you enable, without you needing to switch between apps.

How your Google data is handled

Your Google data is processed locally on your instance of Izzy-Bot. It is never sold or shared with third parties. Google data is used solely to provide the calendar and task features you enable, and is retained only as needed to operate those features.

Invite-only access

Izzy-Bot is not open to the public. Access is granted by invitation, and we may grant or revoke access at our discretion, at any time.
