Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

izzy-bot is a private, invite-only AI chat application operated by Isaac Peel ("we", "us"). izzy-bot uses self-hosted large language models to power its chat functionality. This policy explains what information we collect from users of izzy-bot and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you sign in to izzy-bot, we collect basic account and authentication information, such as your name and email address, from the login provider. We also store the chat messages and any attachments you send through izzy-bot, along with basic usage and log data needed to operate the service.

How we use your information

We use this information to provide the chat service, maintain your conversation history so you can return to past chats, authenticate access to the application, and operate and improve the service.

Self-hosted processing

Chat content you send through izzy-bot is processed by large language models that are self-hosted on infrastructure we operate. Your chat content is not sent to third-party AI providers.

No sale or sharing of data

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties or advertisers.

Data retention

Your information is retained while your account remains active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us as described below.

Your rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, your data by contacting us at isaac@solesonic.com.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Access to izzy-bot is invite-only, which limits exposure of your data to authorized users only.

Children's privacy

izzy-bot is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at isaac@solesonic.com.
